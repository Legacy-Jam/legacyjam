Five experiences

The Festival

Legacy Jam is five experiences in one weekend.

  • Legacy Cypher

    The heart of Battle Day. The Youth Cypher Battle winner and the Pro Cypher Battle winner are each flown to Outbreak Europe 2027 — one of the biggest breaking festivals in the world.

  • Legacy Exchanges

    Curated battle conversations that push the art form to new heights: Canadian breakers matched against international guests, bringing Canadian breaking to the world stage.

  • Legacy Talks

    Real conversations about the culture, in partnership with Unity and sponsored by Sheridan College.

    • Building a Life in Breaking — sustainable careers in the culture
    • Passing It Down — mentorship and the next generation

  • Legacy Workshops

    Learn directly from world-class breakers, in partnership with Expand the Cypher. Three workshops, all levels welcome.

  • Legacy Party

    Saturday night after the battles — DJ Mensa × Cypher Playground. Included with the Full Weekend Pass.

Our purpose

Why Legacy Jam

Breaking is passed down — from teacher to student, from cypher to cypher, from one generation to the next. Legacy Jam exists to keep that chain alive: to celebrate the people who built Canadian breaking, invest in the youth who will carry it forward, and put Canadian breakers on the world stage. We do this for the culture — bringing back the cypher and curating exchanges that inspire across generations.

Every part of the weekend is built around that purpose — a battle format that pairs teachers with students, youth prizes that send the next generation to Outbreak Europe, exchanges that connect Canada to the international scene, and talks and workshops that pass on the knowledge behind the moves.

The crew

Legacy Jam is produced by a crew of Toronto breakers with decades in the culture — organizers, teachers and competitors who came up through this city's cyphers and have spent their careers building spaces for the next generation, including through Unity Charity and Toronto's community jam scene. This is the event we wish we had coming up. Now it's yours.
