Our purpose

Why Legacy Jam

Breaking is passed down — from teacher to student, from cypher to cypher, from one generation to the next. Legacy Jam exists to keep that chain alive: to celebrate the people who built Canadian breaking, invest in the youth who will carry it forward, and put Canadian breakers on the world stage. We do this for the culture — bringing back the cypher and curating exchanges that inspire across generations.

The festival

Six experiences, one weekend, all built around that purpose:

  • Legacy Cypher — the heart of Battle Day; Youth & Pro Cypher winners fly to Outbreak Europe 2027.
  • Legacy Generations — teacher & student battle 2v2, $2,500 cash on the line.
  • Legacy Exchanges — Canadian breakers battle international guests.
  • Legacy Talks — real conversations on mentorship and building a life in breaking.
  • Legacy Workshops — four workshops with world-class breakers, Sunday.
  • Legacy Party — Saturday night, DJ Mensa × Cypher Playground.

Who’s behind it

MEC Crew

Legacy Jam is produced by MEC Crew, a Toronto breaking crew built inside the city’s cyphers over years of battling, teaching and throwing jams. MEC members have judged, hosted, DJed and competed at events across Canada, and have put just as much work into the next generation — through workshops, mentorship and organizations like Unity Charity — as they have on the floor themselves. Legacy Jam is MEC’s biggest undertaking yet: the event we wish we had coming up, built by the people who lived every part of the culture it celebrates.

The organizing team

Piecez leads the team, bringing years of experience as the founder of Unity Charity and EPIC Leadership and a career spent building youth programming across Toronto. B Bad has kept the city’s cyphers moving as a DJ for over a decade, the sound behind countless jams and battles. Boobjester is a Canadian b-boy legend turned MC and host, a fixture at events across the country. Switch B rounds out the crew as an MC and community builder, always looking out for who comes next. Together, MEC has put as much work into the classroom and the community as into the floor — Legacy Jam is that work brought to one stage.
