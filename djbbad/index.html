Meet Your DJ

B Bad

Andel James — known on stage as B Bad — has been a b-boy since 1999 and started DJing in 2010, blending hip-hop, R&B, funk, breaks and soul for some of breaking’s biggest stages: Red Bull BC One, Freestyle Session, Breaking Canada and The Notorious I.B.E. Since 2007 he’s used music to connect with youth through Unity Charity, and in 2017 he founded the 2U4U collective.

A wedding dance floor is the highest-stakes crowd there is — one shot, no do-overs. That’s exactly the kind of pressure B Bad has trained for since 1999, and it’s why couples across Toronto and the GTA trust him to read the room and keep it moving, from grandma’s favourite song to the last dance of the night.

  • Battle-tested crowd reading. Decades of live-reading high-stakes rooms means your dance floor never goes cold.
  • Real range. Fluent across genres and cultures, not locked to one playlist template.
  • Personal, not a roster. You book Andel — the same DJ from your first call to your last dance.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book?

Popular Saturdays in wedding season (May–October) tend to book 9–12 months out. That said, reach out even if your date is close — availability opens up.

Do you take song requests and do-not-play lists?

Both, always. Every couple fills out a planning questionnaire ahead of the wedding covering must-plays, do-not-plays, and anything special (first dance, cultural traditions, family favourites).

Do you MC as well as DJ?

Yes — full MC service is included in every package: introductions, timeline cues, and announcements throughout the day.

What if you’re already booked for our date?

Once your deposit and contract are signed, your date is exclusively yours — no other weddings are booked alongside it.

Do you bring backup equipment?

Yes. Redundant sound gear travels to every wedding, so a single equipment issue never becomes your problem on the day.

Do you travel outside Toronto?

Absolutely — GTA and destination weddings across Ontario. A travel fee may apply outside the immediate GTA; this is confirmed in your custom quote.

Can we meet before booking?

Of course. Every inquiry starts with a free consult call — no pressure, no obligation, just a conversation about your day.

What’s the deposit and payment schedule?

A deposit secures your date, with the balance due before the wedding. Exact terms are laid out clearly in your contract.
